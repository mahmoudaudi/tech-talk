Introduction

What is Robotics?

Robotics is the branch of engineering and computer science that designs, builds, and programs machines capable of carrying out tasks automatically — bridging the physical and digital worlds. At its heart is a microcontroller: the brain that reads sensors and drives actuators.

Arduino Uno

Open-source microcontroller. Simple C-like language, massive community. Perfect for beginners.

ESP32

Built-in Wi-Fi & Bluetooth. Powerful and affordable. Ideal for IoT robots.

Raspberry Pi

Single-board computer running a full OS. Great for vision, AI, and complex logic.

My Story

How It All Started

01

Two years ago, as a first-year CS student, I joined a robotics workshop at Brave Organization — with zero hardware experience.

02

My very first build: a simple LED circuit controlled by code. No motors, no sensors — just a light blinking on command.

03

That moment changed everything. Seeing code control real hardware made programming feel tangible, powerful, and meaningful.

Programming stopped being abstract — it became something I could touch.

Conclusion

What I Learned

💡

Start simple — even an LED blink is a great beginning. Every expert started there.

Function over looks — our "ugly" robot beat every polished competitor on the field.

🏆

Don't fear competition — even as a beginner, showing up is already half the battle.

Leadership matters — I led the team, and coordination was as important as the code.
